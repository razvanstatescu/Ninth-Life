# Landing page round 2: decisions

Status: **decided** (2026-11-11; V18-V24 added the same day after the first design review, V25-V31 after the
second, `design-review.md` revision 2, V32-V35 in the plan after the third review, `design-review.md` revision 3; they fix design defects and change no product call). The product owner answered "all recommended (1-16), including the ADR-006
amendment in item 1 and the MS-10.8 exception for item 9". The orchestrator added one condition on items 14 and
15 (the drawn privacy wording must match the app's own copy); V14 and V15 record how it is met.

Sources: `docs/research/marketing-site/motion-v2.md`, `box-visuals.md`; the draft and final design in
`design.md` in this folder; ADR-006; `.agents/tasks/marketing-landing/decisions.md` (D1-D15, still binding where
not amended below).

| V25 | **What "reaches the play line" means.** A drawing reaches the play line when part of it is on screen and either its whole box is above the line (its bottom at or above 70% of the viewport height) or, for a drawing too tall for the space above the line, its top is at or above the top of the viewport (`shouldPlay` in `motion.ts`). Two IntersectionObservers call it: the play area (bottom margin −30%, thresholds 0 and 1) and a zero-height top line (bottom margin −100%). The 30% line of V1 is unchanged. Alternative considered: the review's single observer with thresholds 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1; rejected because a box taller than the play area keeps a constant ratio while it covers it, so no callback comes when its top passes 0. | Design phase (second review, finding 2); keeps V1's line and purpose | With "top reaches the line" the How step 1 keys and the proof figure's closing lines played below the fold on phones, the first target. |
| V26 | **How on tall viewports: deferred start.** `howStart` gives `'live'` (picture below the fold), `'deferred'` (picture in view at mount, no step active yet) or `'finished'` (no box, or a step already active). A deferred picture shows its finished state until step 1 first crosses the middle line; then `data-how-live`, `data-how-step="1"` and `data-how-play="1"` are set in one callback and the story plays from step 1. Amends the How part of V19 (MS-8.12, ADR-006 A8). | Design phase (second review, finding 1); V19 was a design-phase decision | On a 1024 × 1366 tablet or a 1920 × 1200 monitor the picture is in view at load, and V19 meant How never played there.
