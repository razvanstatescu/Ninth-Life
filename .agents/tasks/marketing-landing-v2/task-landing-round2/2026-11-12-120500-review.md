### The proof chip swap

Each word row puts two layers in one `.site-cell` with `justify-items: start` (`site.css:205`):

```
.site-cell.site-proof__chip
 ├─ role('in',  CHIP[row])  → ProofChip:  [icon] word code   (nl-chip--match / --reject)
 └─ role('out', CHIP[row])  → typing chip:        word        (nl-chip--typing, no icon, no code)
CHIP = [9, 10, 11] on the 240 ms sweep step
```

`ProofChip` renders the icon before `.nl-chip__word` for every state except `typing`, so the two words are not aligned. Both layers fade over `motion-state` (200 ms) starting at the same step, so each row passes through a half-opacity overlap that reads "water r 92837" (`screens/r3-proof3-1280x800-2300ms.png`, `r3-proof3-390x844-2300ms.png`). Three rows one step apart give about 700 ms of doubled text, right before the closing lines at k12. Every play shows it, and with motion on this is what the visitor sees just as the figure makes its point. The user asked for this figure to be researched and improved visually, which is why the three reviewers and the aggregator made it blocking. Aligning the words with a spacer keeps the step timing and the 4 s cap. Fading the typing chip out one step earlier is simpler, but it leaves a 240 ms gap with no chip in the row. Fading the real chip in one step later moves row 3 onto k12 with the closing lines. (confirmed)

### Layout gaps still open

At 320 CSS px without touch emulation the page is 305px wide and the switch stage 225px, so the 240px switch panel loses 15px on the right (the panel edge and key 9's scan ring). How step 1 loses 7px. No text is cut. MS-7.7 says a drawing fits its stage, and `site.mjs` misses this because its narrow sizes use overlay scrollbars. (confirmed in the a11y screenshots `mlv2-narrow-320x640-*`; no fix in `site.css`.) The offline pill and the tablet proof panel are one-rule fixes in the same file, and both are still unchanged (`site.css:366`, `site.css:466`). (confirmed)

### Test coverage

Unit tests cover the role classes, the build-time data, `shouldPlay` boundaries, `splitInitialPlay`, `activeStep`, `howStart`, every play observer and the per-card leads against the 4 s cap. `site.mjs` checks that all 17 drawings play in view at 4 widths in both engines, plus flicks, entering from above, reduced motion and layout shift. Not tested: the alignment of the two proof chip layers during the swap, 320 px with a classic scrollbar, real nav-link jumps and scroll restoration, screen readers' spoken output, a real Windows contrast theme, Firefox, and real phones and tablets.
