How it works

  1. 1

    Press one key per letter

    H and I share key 4, so "hi" is 4 4. No tapping a key three times, no code to learn.

  2. 2

    Pick the sentence you meant

    Sentences appear as you type. Ninth Life can use what the other person just said to put the likely one first.

  3. 3

    Hear it out loud

    Your browser's voice speaks it. The dictionary keeps working when the connection drops.

Every word is checked against your keys

A language model can suggest sentences. It cannot put words in your mouth. Ninth Life turns every suggested word back into keys and drops anything that does not match, so it is never spoken.

Works the way you type

Keyboard

Number keys or the numpad. Enter speaks.

Touch

Keys from 72 to 128 pixels. No swipes, no long presses.

One switch

Row and column scanning, from 0.4 to 5 seconds a step.

Private

Nothing you say is stored or logged. Saved phrases stay on your device.
